Which coverage should I choose?+

Partial Front protects the front bumper, mirror caps and the leading portions of the hood and fenders. Full Front extends film across the entire hood and front fenders, plus the headlights. Full Vehicle adds the remaining painted body panels. Glass and wheels are not body-film coverage. We confirm the exact fit for your vehicle.

Is PPF the same as ceramic coating?+

No. PPF is a physical film that helps protect covered panels from stone chips and minor abrasion. Ceramic coating is a surface treatment for water repellency and easier cleaning; it does not replace film for rock-chip protection. The free windshield treatment is a ceramic glass treatment, not windshield impact film.

Our team confirms vehicle eligibility, final pricing, applicable taxes, availability and payment or cancellation terms before you pay. Which film and warranty are included?+

Our team will confirm the film specification and written warranty for your vehicle before installation. Warranty terms and exclusions depend on the product. PPF helps reduce damage but cannot prevent every impact.

Can I still protect my car after the snow arrives?+

Yes. PPF is fitted in the shop, and we confirm the installation location, timing and aftercare with your appointment. Existing chips and scratches are not repaired by film, so tell us about any paint damage before installation.
